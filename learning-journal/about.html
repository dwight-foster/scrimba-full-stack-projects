Hello there! My name is Dwight and welcome to my learning journal!

After several weeks of learning in the Fullstack developer course I have created a few different solo projects and entered into some new roles. I am excited to share those updates with everyone.

How I stay committed to learning

I have always enjoyed learning. Whether it was a new coding language, a new math branch, or a new sport. I enjoy challenging myself and learning useful skills that I can apply to my work or social life.

While that is fun you also must remember to break up your time and spend it doing other things you love as well like hanging out with friends or family. Only then can you truly see the results of your work.

How I got started

I started with the basics and then gradually made my way to more complicated topics. I started learning python in middle school and high school. Then throughout college my classes were in java. Both languages are extremely helpful in the real world but I always wanted to learn web dev as well.

That is when I decided to start the full stack path. This path was tailored very well to what I wanted to learn and ramped me up slowly.
